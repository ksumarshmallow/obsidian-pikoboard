## Journal editing and attachments

- Edit journal entries in place, with visible Save and Cancel controls. Enter saves; Shift+Enter inserts a line break.
- Search notes and files by typing `[[`, with full paths in the suggestions.
- Attach files while editing an entry, or drag files into a journal editor or task description.
- Switch journal order between newest first and oldest first.

## Next-step progress

- See completed-item counts on next-step checklists.
- Completing the final checklist item records the step in the journal. Plain-text steps have a Complete step button.
- Automatic completion events have a compact appearance, with expandable checklists and an immediate Undo action.

## Task-window improvements

- One scrolling area replaces nested scrolling in the task window.
- Journal editors grow with their content, and new entries are written above history.
- Opening Edit brings the start of the message into view. Active edits are preserved during history refreshes.

Requires Obsidian 1.14.4 or newer.
